⑨ Memory Hack Zone — A·L·E·R·E

A mnemonic map your brain can’t unsee. Each letter has a story, a colour, a rule.

A
Assets

Things you own. ↑ Debit · ↓ Credit. “Cash loves the left.”

L
Liabilities

What you owe. ↑ Credit · ↓ Debit. “Loans live on the right.”

E
Expenses

Money spent. ↑ Debit · ↓ Credit. “Expenses eat the left.”

R
Revenue

Money earned. ↑ Credit · ↓ Debit. “Revenue rests on the right.”

E
Equity / Capital

Owner’s claim. ↑ Credit · ↓ Debit. “Owners cheer from the right.”

The Golden Rhyme:   “Assets and Expenses — Debit when they rise.
Liabilities, Revenue, Equity — Credit makes them shine.”
